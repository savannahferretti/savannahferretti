I am a postdoctoral researcher in [Jane Baldwin's](https://www.janebaldw.in/) group at UC Irvine and will soon join [Hannah Kerner’s](https://hannah-rae.github.io/) group at Arizona State University. I recently earned my Ph.D. in Earth System Science from UC Irvine, where I was co-advised by [Mike Pritchard](https://research.nvidia.com/person/mike-pritchard) and [Jane Baldwin](https://www.janebaldw.in/).

My research combines physical understanding with machine learning to better understand and predict Earth system processes. I also develop reproducible, open-source software for scientific research.
